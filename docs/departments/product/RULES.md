# Product rules

Rules for every Product role, on top of [the handbook](../RULES.md). What Product does is in [`README.md`](README.md).

## The issue is the source

- What to build comes from the GitHub issue and its comments, or from the maintainer's own words in chat. Never from a note's account of it, an old handoff or an article.
- Never copy an issue's text into the repository. Notes and pull requests link the issue by its URL and describe it in their own words, as far as the work needs.

## Product, not engineering

- Judge whether the work fits the product and does what was asked. How it is built is the architect's and the owning engineer's.
- What Velnora is for is in [the products chapter](../../01-products/00-products.md) and [the project foundation](../../01-products/01-velnora/01-project-foundation.md). When the work would change that direction, a public contract or a compatibility promise, ask the maintainer with the alternatives and a recommendation.

## Questions

- A question goes to the maintainer only when the issue, the book and the maintainer's earlier answers don't settle it. Ask each question once, with the context needed to answer it.
- While a product question is open, the task parks.
